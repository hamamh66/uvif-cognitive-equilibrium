\title{UVIF: A Nature-Aligned Framework for Intelligence,\\[0.4em]
  Stability, and Long-Term Adaptation\\
  Toward Variational Equilibrium as a Universal Principle\\[0.2em]
  of Persistent Intelligent Organization}

\begin{abstract}
Modern artificial intelligence systems are predominantly built around the maximization of scalar objectives. While powerful in narrow settings, this paradigm fosters brittleness, resource exhaustion, and alignment failure over time---properties that contrast sharply with the robustness and longevity of natural systems. This paper introduces the \textbf{Unified Variational Intelligence Framework (\uvif{})}, a nature-aligned theoretical architecture that reconceptualizes intelligence not as optimization toward a fixed target, but as the continuous, constrained balancing of competing forces: an \emph{epistemic pull} driving understanding, a \emph{protective shield} preserving critical internal states, a \emph{computational
    drag} enforcing resource realism, an \emph{efficiency imperative}
    governing action, and a \emph{sustainability field} ensuring long-term
    viability. The organizing principle is \emph{Variational Equilibrium}
    (\(\VEq\))---a dynamic, adaptive steady state that neither freezes a
    system into rigidity nor dissolves it into chaos, but sustains
    purposeful coherence through time. Grounded in variational principles
    from physics, biology, and ecology, \uvif{} provides a unifying
    conceptual lens for biological homeostasis, ecological resilience,
    human cognition, artificial intelligence, and organizational
    management. We articulate the framework's philosophical foundations,
    its formal structure, its comparative advantages over optimization-,
    reinforcement-learning-, and free-energy-based paradigms, and its
    implications for next-generation intelligent systems that must remain
    safe, adaptive, and sustainable across decades of deployment.
\end{abstract}
